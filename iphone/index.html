Arc TV

Install Arc TV on your iPhone

Free. Takes about 20 minutes. You only do this once.

Progress 0 of 8 steps done

Before you start

You need:

  • Your iPhone, with iOS 18 or newer. To check, go to Settings → General → About.
  • Your Mac.
  • The charging cable.
  • Wi-Fi.
  • Your Apple ID. This is the email and password you use for the App Store.

Note: Apple allows 3 sideloaded apps at once on a free Apple ID.

The steps

  1. 1

    Turn on Developer Mode

    Done

    Where: iPhone

    1. Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → scroll to the bottom → Developer Mode → turn it ON.
    2. The phone restarts.
    3. After it restarts, tap "Turn On" and type your passcode.
  2. 2

    Get LocalDevVPN (free)

    Done

    Where: iPhone

    1. Tap the button below to open LocalDevVPN in the App Store.
    2. Install it.
    3. Do not open it yet.
    Open LocalDevVPN in the App Store
  3. 3

    Get iloader (free)

    Done

    Where: Mac

    1. Tap the button below to download iloader on your Mac.
    2. Open it.
    3. If the Mac says it can't open it, right-click the app and choose Open.
    Download iloader on your Mac
  4. 4

    Connect the phone

    Done

    Where: Mac + iPhone

    1. Plug the iPhone into the Mac with the cable.
    2. On the phone, tap Trust and type the passcode.
  5. 5

    Install SideStore with iloader

    Done

    Where: Mac

    1. In iloader, sign in with your Apple ID. Then tap "Install SideStore".
    2. Wait until it says it is done. If it asks about the pairing file, say yes.
    3. Then unplug the cable.
  6. 6

    Trust the app

    Done

    Where: iPhone

    1. Go to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management.
    2. Tap your Apple ID email.
    3. Tap Trust.
  7. 7

    Open SideStore

    Done

    Where: iPhone

    1. Open SideStore.
    2. Sign in with your Apple ID if it asks.

    About SideStore

  8. 8

    Install Arc TV

    Done

    Where: iPhone

    1. Open LocalDevVPN and tap Connect. Keep it connected.
    2. Tap the big button below. It opens SideStore and adds Arc TV.
    3. Then tap Arc TV → Get / Install and wait.
    Add Arc TV to SideStore

    If the button does nothing, copy this address, open SideStore → Sources → the + button → paste it:

    https://raw.githubusercontent.com/divyegoswami/arc-tv-releases/main/source.json

    Done!

Keep Arc TV working: refresh every week

Apple stops the app from opening after 7 days unless it is refreshed. This is normal, not a fault.

To refresh:

  1. Open LocalDevVPN → Connect.
  2. Open SideStore → My Apps → Refresh All.
  3. Wait for the green tick.

Tip: keep LocalDevVPN connected and Wi-Fi on. SideStore then tries to refresh by itself in the background.

Open SideStore

New versions of Arc TV

  1. Open SideStore → My Apps.
  2. If an Update button shows next to Arc TV, tap it.

Arc TV also says when a new version is out (Settings → Updates) and opens SideStore for you.

Arc TV does not update fully by itself. One tap is needed.

If something goes wrong

  • Check that LocalDevVPN says Connected.
  • Check that Wi-Fi is on.
  • Close and reopen SideStore.
  • If Arc TV won't open, do the weekly refresh.
  • If you are still stuck, send a photo of the screen to the person who sent you this page.

Arc TV is not made by or connected to Apple. SideStore and iloader are separate free projects.
